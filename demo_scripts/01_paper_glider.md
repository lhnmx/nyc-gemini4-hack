# Demo 1: Paper Glider

**Best for:** Fast warm-up / baseline demo showing end-to-end YouTube research → structured steps → narrated chaptered POV film.

## Prompt (Copy & Paste)

- **Your project:**
  ```text
  Fold a paper glider
  ```
- **Supplies you have (optional):**
  ```text
  paper
  ```
  *(Or leave blank to show `Origami Paper` / printer paper matched in Shopify.)*

## CLI Command

```bash
uv run python -m nyc_gemini4_hack.cli "Fold a paper glider" --owned "paper"
```

## Demo Flow & Talking Points

1. **Intake**: Enter `Fold a paper glider` with `paper` already owned.
2. **YouTube Research + Decisions API**: Point out how CraftPOV finds real YouTube folding tutorials and filters out intro/fluff segments automatically.
3. **Plan & Materials**: Show the concise step-by-step plan and materials checklist with `paper` marked as owned.
4. **Narrated POV Film**: Play the chaptered first-person film and click a chapter to jump/replay a fold step while the written instructions track playback.
